# CPTS Notes and Methodology

> **My personal notes, methodology, and resources from preparing for and passing the HTB Certified Penetration Testing Specialist (CPTS) exam.**

## Introduction

Hello, I'm **Omer Ulusoy**.

I earned my **CPTS certification after 4 months of studying** for it.

Some people might be shocked that I earned it in such a short time, but I'm a fast learner and I was consistently spending around **5 hours per day** studying the Modules and working through the practical exercises.

The CPTS exam is **medium to advanced-level** and definitely not an easy exam. I had to complete the exam in around **80 hours**, including the time required to write the final report.

After submitting my report, I received my certification in just **4 hours**.
